# Ontario Gym and Fitness Studio Permit Checklist Generator - Ontario

**City:** Mississauga | **Area:** 2400 sq ft | **Design occupant load (members + staff):** 45

> Preliminary checklist generated with the free open-source Ontario Gym and Fitness Studio Permit Checklist Generator by Dust 2 Engineering Inc., Mississauga, Ontario. Verify every item with your designer and the local building department before submitting.

## 1. Application basics (always required)
- [ ] Municipal building permit application form, completed and signed by the owner or authorized agent
- [ ] Schedule 1: Designer Information form for each qualified designer on the project
- [ ] Owner authorization if the applicant is not the property owner, plus the lease for a tenant fit-out if the city asks for it

## 2. Architectural drawings
- [ ] Floor plan showing the equipment layout, training zones, change rooms, washrooms and offices
- [ ] Occupant load calculation on the plan: exercise areas carry a high occupant load, which drives exit widths and washroom counts
- [ ] Barrier-free entrance, path of travel and barrier-free washroom where required
- [ ] Exit signs, emergency lighting and fire separations from neighbouring units shown on plan
- [ ] Floor finishes schedule: resilient flooring in training areas, waterproof finishes in wet areas
- [ ] Sign permit is separate from the building permit - apply for it separately

## 3. Ventilation and HVAC
- [ ] HVAC drawings: heating, cooling and ventilation sized for exercise areas, which need much more fresh air than office space
- [ ] Humidity control for showers and high-occupancy training areas
- [ ] Washroom and change room exhaust shown with equipment schedules

## Showers and change rooms
- [ ] Plumbing drawings: showers, washroom fixtures counted from the occupant load, floor drains in change rooms and shower areas
- [ ] Waterproofing and sloped floors in shower areas shown in the details
- [ ] Barrier-free shower and barrier-free washroom where required
- [ ] Backflow preventer where required, and hot water capacity for peak shower demand

## 4. Plumbing and electrical
- [ ] Plumbing drawings: washrooms, drinking fountain or bottle filler, mop sink and hot water supply
- [ ] Electrical drawings: panels and receptacles for rows of cardio equipment, lighting and emergency lighting
- [ ] Confirm the existing electrical service can take the added equipment and HVAC load

## 5. Other approvals (not part of the building permit)
- [ ] Business licence from the city for the fitness facility, after the permit is closed
- [ ] If a pool or hot tub is planned, it is a separate regulated design (public pools are inspected by public health) - it is not covered by this checklist
- [ ] Check your lease and the condominium rules for noise, vibration and operating-hour limits before you sign

## Before you submit
- [ ] One combined PDF set: architectural, mechanical, plumbing, electrical and structural (if any), all consistent with each other
- [ ] All drawing sets use the same address, unit number, floor area and counts
- [ ] Designs that require an engineer must be stamped by a licensed Ontario P.Eng.; BCIN designer information where applicable
- [ ] Book a pre-consultation or zoning check with the city if the use or layout is not clearly permitted
